Meet **Lodi Reader** — your personal storyteller, built for people who want to *listen* instead of read.

Imagine turning any book into an effortless, immersive audio experience. Whether it’s a `.txt` file or a beautifully formatted `.epub`, Lodi Reader brings your library to life with smooth, natural text-to-speech that adapts to your rhythm.

**Your Library, Your Way**
Load books instantly and organize them on a clean, intuitive **Shelf** that remembers exactly where you left off. No more scrolling, no more guessing — just pick up and continue.

**Seamless Listening Experience**
Life interrupts — Lodi Reader doesn’t. It automatically pauses when another app takes over audio and resumes when you're ready. Using earphones? Control everything with simple **play and pause buttons** without even touching your phone.

**Designed for Real Life**
Falling asleep to a story? Set a **sleep timer** and relax. If you drift off, Lodi gently stops — and when you wake, just **shake your device** to continue. A subtle bell confirms it worked, so you’re never left wondering.

**Smooth, Continuous Reading**
Enjoy uninterrupted narration with **continuous pages**, creating a flowing, audiobook-like experience that keeps you immersed chapter after chapter.
